Services

Force-plate testing

Jump, land, and move on Vald ForceDecks while we measure what the eye can't see — power, asymmetry, and how quickly you produce force.

  • Jump height & explosiveness
  • Peak power, relative to bodyweight
  • Peak force & left / right asymmetry
  • Squat-to-jump transition

One-on-one and small-group training built from your test results — strength, power, speed, and movement quality, progressed over time.

How it works

Three steps. No mystery.

  1. 1

    Test

    A short, guided testing session on the force plates. No preparation needed — just show up ready to move.

  2. 2

    Get your report

    Within 48 hours, you get a clear, readable report: your numbers, how they compare to your own average, and what changed since your last test.

  3. 3

    Train to the plan

    We build training around what the test showed — and re-test over time so you can see progress, not just feel it.

Reports are designed to be read in about 60 seconds by an athlete, a parent, a coach, or a clinician.

FAQ

Common questions

What is force-plate testing?

Force plates measure the force you put into the ground when you jump, land, and move. That tells us about power, speed of force production, and differences between your left and right sides — things that are hard to see with the eye alone.

Do I need to be an elite athlete?

No. Testing is useful for developing athletes, returning athletes, and older adults alike — the numbers are always compared to you, not to an elite standard.

How long does testing take?

Individual testing is a short, guided session. Team testing days are scheduled by roster size — ask us and we'll give you a time plan for your group.

What do I get after testing?

A readable report within 48 hours, with your key metrics, comparisons to your own average, and notes on what to work on next.

Can I share my report with my coach or PT?

Yes — that's the point. Reports are made to be shared, and we're happy to walk through them with your coach or clinician.
